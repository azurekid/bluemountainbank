Security Best Practices

Strong Passwords

  • Use at least 12 characters
  • Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
  • Avoid personal information
  • Use unique passwords for each account

Safe Internet Practices

  • Avoid public Wi-Fi for banking
  • Always log out completely
  • Check for "https://" in the URL
  • Keep your browser updated

Phishing Awareness

  • We never ask for passwords via email
  • Verify suspicious calls by calling us directly
  • Check sender email addresses carefully
  • Don't click suspicious links

Mobile Security

  • Use official app store downloads only
  • Enable screen locks and biometrics
  • Log out when finished
  • Keep your app updated

Current Security Alerts

Phishing Email Campaign Alert

We've identified fraudulent emails claiming to be from Blue Mountain Bank asking customers to "verify their account information." These emails are NOT from us.

What to do: Delete the email immediately. If you've already clicked on links or provided information, contact us immediately at 1-800-BMB-BANK.

Fraudulent Text Messages

Scammers are sending text messages claiming your account has been compromised and asking you to call a fake number.

Remember: We will never ask you to provide personal information via text message. Always call our official number to verify any suspicious communications.
